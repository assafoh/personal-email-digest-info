Privacy policy

The automation reads message metadata and content from Gmail accounts authorized by its operator. It uses this information to select and summarize important messages. It does not change Gmail messages or settings.

OAuth credentials, review reports, checkpoints, and delivery records are stored on the operator’s computer. During a run, email content is processed by Codex to prepare the digest. Selected summaries are sent via WhatsApp to the operator’s private group. This website does not receive email content or OAuth credentials.

To stop access, revoke the app in your Google Account’s third-party connections. The operator can delete local records and WhatsApp copies separately.
